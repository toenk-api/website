Our Story

In 2025, we saw a gap: Chinese AI models like DeepSeek, Qwen, and GLM were reaching GPT-4 class performance at a fraction of the cost — but global developers struggled to access them with low latency. Meanwhile, Chinese enterprises building AI applications faced significant compliance hurdles when using overseas models like GPT-4 and Claude.

TOENK was born to solve both sides of this equation. We're not just an API proxy — we're the infrastructure layer that makes AI work across borders, securely and compliantly.

What Makes Us Different

Bidirectional Bridge: We're the only platform that gives global developers low-latency access to DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, and Kimi — while simultaneously giving Chinese enterprises compliant access to GPT-4, Claude, and Gemini.

Security by Default: Content filtering, PII redaction, AI watermarking, and audit logging are built into every API call. Not add-ons — core features.

Smart Routing: Our context-aware engine automatically picks the optimal model for each request, cutting API costs by 40-60% without sacrificing quality.
