Frequently Asked Questions

How does billing work?
You're charged per token at the end of each month. No minimum commitment. You can set spending limits in the console to avoid surprises.
What's included in the free tier?
The Starter plan gives you 1,000 free credits per month (~$1 worth of API calls) and access to all models at standard rates.
How does Smart Routing save me money?
Our Smart Router automatically selects the most cost-effective model for your request while maintaining quality. For example, it routes simple queries to fast models and complex ones to advanced models — saving 40-60%.
Can I pay in CNY?
Yes. We support CNY billing for Chinese customers. Contact our sales team for details.
What about data privacy?
We don't store your prompts or completions beyond what's needed for billing. Enterprise plans include data processing agreements and private deployment options.
How do volume discounts work?
Pro plan users get 15% off when monthly spend exceeds $100. Enterprise plans receive custom volume pricing. The more you use, the less you pay per token.
