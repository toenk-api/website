When to Use Each Model

Choose DeepSeek V4 Pro when:

  • You need cost-efficient production at scale
  • Building AI agents with tool calling
  • Chinese-language or bilingual applications
  • Mathematical reasoning and competition-level problems
  • You want open-source flexibility and self-hosting options

Choose GPT-5 when:

  • You need multimodal (vision + audio) capabilities
  • Maximum general reasoning performance regardless of cost
  • Access to OpenAI's ecosystem (Assistants API, etc.)

Choose Claude 4 Sonnet when:

  • Software engineering is your primary use case
  • You need the 200K context window for large codebases
  • Safety and controlled outputs are critical
  • Long-form content generation with consistent quality
