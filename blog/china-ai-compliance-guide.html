Building AI Agents in China: A Practical Compliance Guide

May 17, 20266 min readCompliance · Guide · Enterprise

If you're building AI agents or applications that serve users in China, you face a unique regulatory landscape. China's MLPS 2.0 (等保2.0), generative AI regulations, and data protection laws create requirements that Western platforms simply don't address. Here's how to navigate them — and how TOENK helps.

The Three Regulations You Must Know

1. MLPS 2.0 (网络安全等级保护 2.0)

China's cybersecurity law requires organizations to classify their systems by security level (1-5) and implement corresponding controls. For AI API services, Level 3 is typical. Requirements include network security, host security, application security, data security, and audit logging.

2. Generative AI Service Regulations

In effect since August 2023, these require: content safety filtering, generated content labeling/watermarking, user complaint handling mechanisms, training data compliance, and filing/registration with authorities.

3. Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL)

Similar to GDPR, requires: consent for data collection, data localization (certain categories must stay in China), data subject rights (access, deletion, portability), and cross-border data transfer assessments.
